About Us

Sridevi Emandi

Founder of SreeKML Boutique

Elegance Crafted With Passion

Welcome to SreeKML Boutique, where tradition meets modern elegance. We specialize in premium sarees, bridal collections, maggam work blouses, embroidery designs, and designer wear curated for every special occasion.

Every design reflects timeless craftsmanship, rich fabrics, and attention to detail. Our boutique is dedicated to making every woman feel confident, graceful, and beautiful.

Under the vision of our founder Sridevi Emandi, SreeKML Boutique continues to bring unique fashion collections that celebrate Indian tradition with contemporary style.
